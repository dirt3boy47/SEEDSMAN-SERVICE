01 — Approach

Coastal landscapes,
precisely kept.

Seedsmans looks after established hedges and gardens on coastal properties — the clipped lines, the wind-shaped screens and the larger grounds that need a steady hand through every season.

We keep gardens looking considered without making them feel over-managed, and we price every property by measuring the landscape first.

02 — Services

What we maintain

01

Hedge cutting

Routine and one-off maintenance of established hedges. Every face kept to a clean, even line.

02

Hedge reductions

Controlled reductions and reshaping of mature hedging — height and width brought back without losing the screen.

03

Estate maintenance

Scheduled landscape maintenance for larger residential properties, planned around the seasons.

04

Green waste

Removal and responsible management of the vegetation generated during maintenance.

05 — About Seedsmans

A considered approach to landscape maintenance.

Seedsmans specialises in the care of established hedges and gardens across coastal properties.

Our approach combines skilled hedge work, reliable scheduled maintenance and an understanding of how coastal landscapes grow, weather and change.

We aim to keep gardens looking considered without making them feel over-managed.
