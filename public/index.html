About Seedsmans

Seedsmans cuts, shapes and maintains hedges on coastal properties across the Mornington Peninsula.

Coastal hedges take salt, wind and sandy soil. We cut them to suit how they grow there, keep work areas tidy and take the green waste away.

We measure before we price, so the quote matches the job.
