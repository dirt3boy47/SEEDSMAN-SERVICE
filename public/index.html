Good to know

Questions

How do you work out the price?

We measure your hedges from up-to-date aerial imagery — length, height and depth — and price by the square metre of hedge face we trim (top, front, back and ends). Height, how overgrown it is, the cherry picker for tall hedges and green waste are all shown on the quote.

Do you need to come out first?

Often not — the aerial measuring and any photos you send are usually enough. If something needs a closer look, we’ll arrange a quick visit before we quote.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes. Taking the clippings away can be included in your quote — we work out the volume from the job.

How tall can you go?

Tall hedges are no problem. When a hedge needs it, we bring a cherry picker (an elevated work platform) and it’s itemised on your quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually, as long as we can get to the hedges. Just let us know about gates, gate codes, dogs and parking.

How do I accept the quote?

We send you a link. You can view the quote with its site map on your phone or computer, download the PDF, and accept it with one tap. Then we book you in.

Can you come back regularly?

Yes. After the job we can set a reminder for your next trim — every 3, 4, 6 or 12 months — and get in touch when it’s due.
